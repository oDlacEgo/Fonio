# Fonio - Modern Radio & Multimedia Player 📻🎵🎬

**Fonio** is a versatile and modern Android application designed for high-quality radio streaming, local music playback, and video consumption. Built with the latest Android technologies, it offers a sleek, intuitive, and highly customizable user interface.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📡 Radio Streaming
- **Live Radio**: Add and stream your favorite radio stations from around the world.
- **Instant Recording**: Capture live radio directly to your device with a single tap.
- **Recording Timer**: Set recording limits using a sleek, modern slider.
- **Station Management**: Easily add, edit, move, or remove stations. Export and import your list as JSON.
- **Song History**: Automatically tracks the last 10 unique songs played on the radio.

### 🎵 Audio Player
- **Local Playback**: High-quality playback of your MP3 and other local audio files.
- **Folder Explorer**: Navigate your music library via a familiar folder structure.
- **Wave Visualizer**: A modern, animated sine-wave progress bar that "lives" with your music.
- **Playback Control**: Adjust playback speed (1.0x - 2.0x) and switch between sequential, shuffle, and repeat modes.
- **Resume Feature**: Remembers the last played song and position for each folder.

### 🎬 Video Player
- **Fullscreen Experience**: Immersive video playback with gesture controls (double tap to skip, horizontal drag to exit).
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)**: Keep watching your favorite videos while using other apps.
- **Last Position**: Automatically saves and restores your progress for every video.

### 🛠️ Advanced Tools
- **Sleep Timer**: Elegant slider to schedule automatic playback shutdown with a gentle fade-out.
- **Ambient UI**: Dynamic background artwork with "Ambient Glow" and adjustable blur effects.
- **Modern UI/UX**: Built with **Jetpack Compose** and **Material 3**, featuring glassmorphism effects and smooth animations.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Language**: [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
- **UI Framework**: [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/compose)
- **Design System**: Material 3 (Dynamic Colors supported)
- **Media Engine**: [Media3 / ExoPlayer](https://developer.android.com/guide/topics/media/media3)
- **Image Loading**: [Coil](https://coil-kt.github.io/coil/)
- **Architecture**: Coroutines, Flow, StateFlow
